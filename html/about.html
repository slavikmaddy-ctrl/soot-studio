WHO IS SOOT

Soot Studio opened in 1934 in the attic above Elder Kettle’s cottage. Two cup-headed apprentices, one pot-bellied mentor and a bottomless pot of ink.

We draw the way the old pictures did: pencil first, ink on vellum, paint on glass — one honest frame after another. No tricks, no copying machines. The ink is mixed by hand and the metronome never lies.

What we shoot: title sequences, story-driven shorts, museum screens and the occasional campaign that needs real smoke instead of cheap sparkles.
