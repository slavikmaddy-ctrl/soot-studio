FROM THE LEDGER

The house rules taped above the drawing desks — same ink since 1934.

RULE 1 — PENCIL FIRST

No painting before the gag survives being told to the kettle out loud. If you laugh — it lives.

SIGNED: MUGMAN
RULE 2 — INK ON 24

Every second holds exactly 24 frames. Stretch a gag to 25 and the kettle will hear it squint.

SIGNED: CUPHEAD
RULE 3 — TALK BEFORE THE SHOT

Animators and developers talk before the frame, not after. Borrowed, with thanks, from whoisguilty.com.

SIGNED: THE KETTLE
